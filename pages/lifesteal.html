Game Mode

Lifesteal SMP

Steal hearts from your enemies and grow stronger in this hardcore survival experience.

Lifesteal SMP

Every kill matters - steal hearts and survive

Features

  • Steal hearts on kill - Gain health from defeating enemies
  • Custom enchantments - Unique powerful enchants not found in vanilla
  • Economy & trading - Player-driven marketplace and shops
  • Clans & alliances - Team up with friends to dominate
  • Custom items - Exclusive weapons, armor, and tools
  • PvP zones - Designated combat areas with special rewards
  • Heart crafting - Craft hearts using rare materials
  • Withdrawal system - Store hearts safely in your vault

How to Play

Join the server at play.lifeamc.net and type /lifesteal to enter the Lifesteal world. You start with 10 hearts (20 HP). When you kill another player, you steal one of their hearts and add it to your own health bar. If you lose all your hearts, you'll be temporarily banned until you can craft a heart or receive one from another player!


Commands:

  • /lifesteal - Teleport to Lifesteal world
  • /hearts - Check your current hearts
  • /withdraw - Withdraw a heart item
  • /clan - Manage your clan
