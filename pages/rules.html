Rules

Server Rules

Follow these rules to keep Lifea MC fair, fun, and friendly for everyone.

Play Fair, Have Fun

Breaking these rules will result in warnings, mutes, kicks, or permanent bans depending on severity. If you're unsure about something, ask a staff member!

1

No Cheating or Hacking

Using hacked clients, x-ray, fly hacks, kill aura, or any unfair advantage is strictly prohibited. This includes exploiting bugs or glitches.

2

Respect Everyone

Be respectful to all players and staff. No harassment, bullying, racism, sexism, or hate speech of any kind. Treat others how you want to be treated.

3

No Spam or Advertising

Don't spam chat with repeated messages, caps, or nonsense. Advertising other servers or Discord servers is not allowed.

4

No Griefing in Protected Areas

Respect claimed land and protected areas. Griefing, stealing, or raiding in safe zones is not allowed. PvP zones are fair game.

5

Keep Chat Clean

No excessive swearing, inappropriate content, or NSFW material. Keep the chat family-friendly and appropriate for all ages.

6

No Alt Accounts for Exploits

Using alternate accounts to bypass bans, gain unfair advantages, or exploit systems is prohibited. One player, one account.

7

Listen to Staff

Staff decisions are final. If you disagree with a punishment, appeal it properly through Discord. Arguing with staff will result in additional punishment.

8

No Scamming or IRL Trading

Scamming other players is not allowed. Trading in-game items for real money or items on other servers is prohibited.
