Rules

Keep It Fair

These rules exist so everyone has a good time. They're not complicated — just be a decent person and you'll be fine.

Play Fair, Have Fun

Punishments scale with severity — most first offences get a warning. Repeat or serious violations lead to mutes, kicks, or permanent bans. If you're ever unsure about something, just ask a staff member before doing it.

1

No Cheating or Hacking

Permanent Ban

Hacked clients, x-ray, kill aura, fly hacks, or any unfair advantage — zero tolerance. This includes intentionally exploiting bugs. If you find a bug, report it; don't abuse it.

2

Respect Everyone

Mute / Ban

No harassment, bullying, racism, sexism, or hate speech. Trash talk in PvP is fine — targeted hate is not. Treat others how you'd want to be treated.

3

No Spam or Advertising

Mute

Don't flood chat with repeated messages, excessive caps, or random nonsense. Advertising other servers or Discord servers will get you muted immediately.

4

No Griefing in Protected Areas

Kick / Ban

Respect claimed land and safe zones. Griefing, stealing, or raiding in protected areas is not allowed. PvP zones are fair game — that's what they're there for.

5

Keep Chat Clean

Warning

No excessive swearing or NSFW content. We have younger players on the server — keep it appropriate. A little banter is fine; going overboard is not.

6

No Alt Accounts for Exploits

Permanent Ban

Using alt accounts to bypass bans, farm rewards, or gain any unfair advantage is not allowed. One player, one account. Ban evasion results in a permanent ban on all accounts.

7

Respect Staff Decisions

Warning

Staff decisions are final in-game. If you genuinely believe a punishment was unfair, appeal it calmly through our Discord — don't argue in chat. Arguing publicly makes things worse, not better.

8

No Scamming or IRL Trading

Permanent Ban

Scamming other players is not tolerated. Trading in-game items for real money or items on other servers is also prohibited. Keep all trades within the server economy.

Got a question about the rules?

Don't guess — just ask. Our staff are active on Discord and happy to clarify anything.
