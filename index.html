Lifesteal SMP

Steal hearts from your enemies and grow stronger

Features

  • Steal hearts on kill - gain health from defeating enemies
  • Custom enchantments - unique powerful enchants
  • Economy & trading - player-driven marketplace
  • Clans & alliances - team up with friends
  • Custom items - exclusive weapons and armor
  • PvP zones - designated combat areas

How to Play

Join the server and type /lifesteal to enter the Lifesteal world. Start with 10 hearts and fight other players to steal their hearts. Lose all your hearts and you'll be temporarily banned until you can craft a heart or receive one from another player!

Earth SMP

Build your nation on a real-world map

Features

  • Real-world map - 1:1000 scale Earth terrain
  • Nation system - create and manage your own country
  • Territory wars - conquer land and expand your empire
  • Diplomacy & trade - form alliances and trade routes
  • Custom resources - unique ores per continent
  • World events - server-wide challenges

How to Play

Join the server and type /earthsmp to teleport to Earth. Choose your starting location, claim territory, and build your nation. Form alliances with other nations or wage war to expand your borders!

FFA Arena

Free-for-all combat arena

Features

  • Instant respawn - get back into action immediately
  • Custom kits - choose from 10+ unique loadouts
  • Kill streaks - earn rewards for consecutive kills
  • Ranked leaderboards - compete for the top spot
  • Daily challenges - complete tasks for rewards
  • Multiple arenas - different maps and themes

How to Play

Join the server and type /ffa to enter the arena. Select your kit and start fighting! Earn points for kills and climb the leaderboard. Complete daily challenges to unlock exclusive rewards!
